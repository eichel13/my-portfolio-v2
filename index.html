Undergraduate Thesis Sept 2025 to May 2026

Urban Housing Rent Prediction, Barangay Manggahan

FILE #03

PHILGEOS / UP DILIMAN

OBJECTIVE & SCOPE

Mapped unrecorded building ages to predict fine-scale rental prices in a mixed-income barangay in Pasig City, using a gradient boosting approach.

MY PART & EXPERTISE

Developed a Python algorithm that estimates building construction and completion years from Landsat time series, trained and validated on manually traced construction histories. A rate-based approach outperformed a magnitude-based one. Produced the study maps and ran Moran's I on rent and building age clustering.

FIELD SURVEYING

Academic field projects from the BS Geodetic Engineering program at UP Diliman.

Cadastral

Property survey, Lot 41-C-3-A-19 (Sandiganbayan Building II site)

Relocated and consolidated six parcels, subdivided a 26,835 sq.m. property, and staked corners by total station within 10 cm tolerance.

Control

Geodetic control survey

7-point closed-loop traverse with 1:27,751 REC against a 1:20,000 3rd-order requirement, with least squares adjustment.

Leveling

Differential leveling

Double set-up three-wire circuits to 2 mm closure (2nd order). Computed a reciprocal leveling C-factor of 0.1795.

GNSS

Network and kinematic survey

Closed 6-triangle network observed with Trimble SPS985 and u-blox ZED-F9P, processed in RTKLIB at 31.405 ppm misclosure.

Route

Road design and earthwork, Roces St.

750 m alignment at 120 kph in Civil 3D, with cut/fill from 20 m cross-sections and mass haul diagrams.

Hydro / As-built

Bathymetric and road as-built surveys

UP Lagoon bathymetry (1:21,037 REC) modeled in QGIS. Road as-built traverse at 1:17,918 REC with CAD plans.

Calibration

Total station calibration

Nikon and Sokkia instruments calibrated on 25-100 m baselines to 99.9968% relative accuracy.
